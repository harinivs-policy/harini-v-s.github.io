#DecodeThePolicy

National Geospatial Policy 2022

India opened up its geospatial data. Did it make that data useful to citizens?

From restricted access to a more open geospatial ecosystem, India's policy landscape has changed significantly. But making data available is only one part of the journey. The next challenge is turning access into usability, integration and meaningful public outcomes.

02 / The policy journey

From restriction to openness

India's geospatial policy journey can be understood through two important milestones.

2021

Opening the ecosystem

The liberalised geospatial guidelines and Drone Rules marked a shift towards easing restrictions and enabling wider access and participation.

2022

A common policy framework

The National Geospatial Policy established a broader framework for developing the geospatial sector and improving access to geospatial data and services.

03 / The policy gap

Availability is not the same as impact

A policy can enable data access without automatically ensuring that institutions can use that data effectively or that citizens experience better public services.

The next phase is not just about making data available. It is about making data usable, integrated and relevant to citizen outcomes.

Usability

Can institutions, researchers and local bodies actually use the available data?

Integration

Can geospatial information work across departments, systems and public-service processes?

Citizen impact

Does better access translate into more responsive planning and improved public outcomes?

The central question

The data is flowing. The real win is when a citizen feels the difference.
