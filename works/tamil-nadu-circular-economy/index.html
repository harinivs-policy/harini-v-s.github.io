Tamil Nadu's Circular Economy Investment Policy

Turning Waste Into Value: From Compliance Burden to Industrial Opportunity
Policy Brief 2026 Harini V S
Project overview

From waste management to industrial strategy.

Tamil Nadu's transition towards a circular economy presents an opportunity to connect environmental sustainability with industrial competitiveness. This policy brief examines how investment incentives, recycling infrastructure and stronger institutional mechanisms can help establish circular value chains across key manufacturing sectors.

The analysis considers the policy's investment framework alongside implementation challenges involving MSME participation, extended producer responsibility, quality certification and the integration of informal waste workers. It identifies policy adjustments that could move the focus beyond end-of-life recycling towards resource efficiency, repair, reuse and higher-value manufacturing.

Analytical focus

Three questions shape the analysis.

01

Investment & competitiveness

Can circular production become a source of industrial value, resource security and export readiness?

02

Implementation & inclusion

Can policy instruments reach smaller enterprises and recognise the contribution of informal-sector workers?

03

Regulation & accountability

Can stronger verification, certification and producer responsibility improve the credibility of circular value chains?
