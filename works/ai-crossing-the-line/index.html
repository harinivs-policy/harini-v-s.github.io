AI Crossing the Line

Automating traffic enforcement without automating judgement.

Project overview

A policy memorandum examining the safe deployment of AI agents in India's automated traffic enforcement systems, including CCTV-based detection and integration with e-Challan and Parivahan.

The proposal recommends a deny-by-default runtime sandbox, restricted data access, mandatory human approval, audit protocols and a clear citizen redressal process for erroneous challans.
